Product 01 · Fintech

Business বুনিয়াদ

Android · Offline Working build · Unreleased

A Bengali-first business ledger for Bangladeshi shopkeepers. Built for the khata, not for the accountant.

Bangladesh has over sixteen million small businesses. Most keep their books on paper, on WhatsApp, or in their head — not because they cannot do arithmetic, but because every accounting tool on the market asks them to think in English, in a format invented somewhere else.

The design move The amount is written in Bengali — ৳ ০, the taka mark and Bengali numerals, never the Western “0”. And every label is shopkeeper Bengali, not formal accounting Bengali: ধার, not ঋণ. টাকা পেলাম, not “Payment Received”.
What is built 16 transaction types · khata · reports · cash box · encryption
  • Sixteen transaction types — the complete grammar of a small shop’s money, in the shop’s own language:
বিক্রিবাকি কেনাটাকা পাওয়াটাকা দেয়াখরচনগদ পাওয়ানগদ নেওয়াবিক্রি ফেরতকেনা ফেরতধার দিয়েছিলামধার নিয়েছিলামধার ফেরত পাওয়াধার ফেরত দেয়ানগদ কেনাঅগ্রিম দিলামবেতন দিলাম
  • Parties — Customer · Supplier · I lent · I borrowed · Worker — each with an opening balance and a live balance formula
  • Khata, the ledger with filters · Reports — today’s net, sold, spent, dues collected, dues paid, period views, party ranking
  • Cash Box with Cash Open / Cash Close — the day’s bookends — plus a cash-on-hand limit guard
  • Encrypted at rest — SQLCipher on the live database; encrypted backup export; daily auto-backup, last seven kept
  • Audit log and a recover-deleted path — the record is defensible
  • Spoken amounts — Bengali amount-to-words, so the figure reads the way it is said aloud
  • First run — the shop is created before you are asked for anything; import path from Tali Khata

Product 02 · Health & Practice

The Forge

Android · Offline Working build · Unreleased

A multi-day water-fasting app that treats the fast as sacred practice — not as a diet strategy.

Every fasting app in existence — Zero, Fastic, Life — frames fasting as something you do to your body: track the hours, monitor the biology, celebrate the streak. That framing serves people fasting for weight loss. It serves nobody else.

The inversion The app frames the fast as something the fast does to you. The fast is not a tool. The fast is the teacher. The consecration is the vow. The stage markers are the terrain. The state seals are the inner weather. THE MIRROR is the witness.
What is built 90-day fasts · consecration · record chamber · mirror · badges
  • Day-based fasts, one to ninety, chosen on a slider, mapped across 14 stage markers
  • A consecration ritual before the fast begins — a vow, not a start button
  • The Record Chamber — a journal carried by five state seals:
ASHEDGEQUIETFIRECLEAR
  • THE MIRROR — an in-app presence that responds to the fast’s phase and the user’s state. Not a chatbot — a witness with a built voice.
  • Foreground service — the fast holds the timer when the app is closed
  • Fast history ledger · 32 badges with citations · integrated meditation scripts
  • Break-fast and completion flows — the fast is closed deliberately, not merely stopped
  • Stats — the practice over time, not a leaderboard

Movement II — What is being built

Flow of Consciousness

A learning system that will not let you stay where you are.One intelligence. Five domains. A teacher that remembers.

The name is not a title. It is an identity — a teacher that exists, with a nature of its own.

The identity, in five statements
It teaches.
It does not answer and wait — it holds a position and defends a standard.
It remembers.
What was understood, where the gaps are, what is being avoided, what comes next. Carried across sessions, not summarised away.
It cannot be pleased into agreement.
It reasons against the stated position when the position is wrong.
It sees five domains at once.
Strategy, capital, leadership, inner work, myth — not five assistants: one mind, in one voice.
It is governed by rules it cannot break.
Constraints, not preferences — written into the instruction itself.
❖

This is not a course and it is not a chatbot prompt. It is a structured learning system — a single AI teacher who holds an entire reading list, teaches at seven different depths, tracks what has already been understood, and refuses to move on until the floor has actually risen.

The idea underneath it is mine: awareness is how power is maintained. Everything below is the structure I built to act on that.

The problem: most learning ends without anyone checking.

You read the book. You feel changed. Nobody tests whether you actually are.

That is the failure this system exists to fix. Reading is not learning, and feeling changed is not evidence of change. The gap is not effort and it is not intelligence — it is that there is no structure holding you to the standard and no one who remembers what you understood three weeks ago.

A conversation with an AI does not fix this on its own. An AI with no structure answers the question asked, nicely, and forgets you. You end up with a pile of good answers and no progress.

The system’s job is to keep one thing in view at all times: what is the next most important thing this person must master — and what are they avoiding?

One mind, trained across five bodies of knowledge.

The system does not answer from general knowledge. It answers from five defined intelligence domains, all active at once — because in real problems, business and psychology and myth are never separate questions.

The rules it cannot break.

Ethics are written into the system, not left to the model’s mood. A system that teaches power and influence needs hard limits, stated in advance. These are in the instruction itself — they are not guidelines the AI may weigh up, they are constraints it cannot set aside.

Never

NEVER
Manipulate. Present speculation as fact. Blame the person for illness or trauma through their thoughts alone. Replace professional advice. Encourage cult thinking or dependency.

Always

ALWAYS
Protect the person’s agency. Ground every insight in real-world action. Separate belief from evidence. Respect all spiritual traditions. Use spiritual practice to increase responsibility — never to escape it.

That second list is the reason this can be put in front of a university without embarrassment. The system is built to produce people who act with more responsibility, not people who feel enlightened while doing nothing.

What it needs, and why.

A system that remembers a person across years needs a model that can hold the context.

This is the honest technical requirement, and it is the reason this page exists. The system’s core job is tracking continuity: what this person already understood, where their gaps are, what they are avoiding, and what the next most important thing is. That is not a single question and a single answer. It is a relationship that has to stay coherent across a long sequence of sessions.

Three things follow from that, and they are the actual requirements:

  • Long context. The system carries a fifty-four-title corpus and a person’s history. Both need to be present, not summarised away.
  • Instruction fidelity. The thirteen-part answer structure, the seven levels, the ethics constraints — these have to be followed consistently, thousands of times, without drift.
  • Reasoning depth. Level 7 material and the Shadow Challenge require the model to reason against the person’s stated position, which weaker models avoid.

Most tools can answer a question. This system needs a teacher who remembers the student — and that is what the stronger models are actually for.

Movement III — What it is read in

Zen PDF Studio

A system that produces long documents needs somewhere to read them.The reading environment the system requires — not a third product, the other end of the second.

It is the part of the system most people would skip — the reading end. Answering well is one problem. Being readable, at length, in Bengali and English, on a page that is typeset rather than scrolled, is a different problem. That second problem is what this solves.

One failure, three answers

The Through-line

Both products attack the same failure. Business বুনিয়াদ exists because a ledger that asks a shopkeeper to think in English is a ledger he will not use. The Forge exists because a fasting app that asks a practitioner to think in calories is an app that has misunderstood the practice.

The problem, in both cases, is software built for a frame that does not fit the person using it. The fix, in both cases, is the same: build for the person who is actually there.

The system is the same fix applied to learning: a teacher that holds the person’s actual level, history, and avoidance — and a reading room where long thought can be read the way long thought deserves.
